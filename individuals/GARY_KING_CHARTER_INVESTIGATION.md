# Gary King — "two diffent entities 10 4"

*In August 2014 Jeffrey Epstein's staff arranged a private jet for New Mexico's sitting attorney general. Epstein told his accountant the campaign "can pay 11.6" and that "we can arrange to pay through two diffent entities 10 4." While the booking was being set up, his assistant circulated the Attorney General's state office, not the campaign, as King's contact information. A week before the flight, two Epstein companies each gave King's campaign $5,200, which is $10,400. The campaign paid the operator $22,060.90 three days before takeoff and reported refunding the companies after a newspaper found them. The documents fit a split fare. They do not show who at the campaign, if anyone, understood it that way.*

---

## What was already known, and what this adds

The outline of the September 8, 2014 flight has been public since February 2026. [NM Political Report](https://nmpoliticalreport.com/2026/02/17/records-show-gary-king-deb-haaland-used-epstein-linked-jet-during-2014-campaign/) and the [Albuquerque Journal](https://www.abqjournal.com/news/deb-haaland-rode-on-jet-chartered-by-epstein-in-2014-for-running-mate-gary-king/2982850) established that **Gary King**, then attorney general and the Democratic nominee for governor, flew with running mate **Deb Haaland** on a Hawker chartered through Epstein's company JEGE LLC, that King's campaign later listed a $22,060 payment to the charter operator, and that Epstein-linked companies were giving to the campaign in the same weeks. The Journal printed Epstein's "11.6 … 10 4" email verbatim without interpreting it. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/politics-news/as-new-mexico-investigates-questions-are-raised-about-epsteins-links-to-the-powerful-4574044) added in March that King's campaign returned $35,600 to Epstein companies three days after the story broke, a line Part V below tests against the campaign's own filings.

This report reads the same emails against the charter operator's written quote, which the coverage did not cite, and against the trip sheet, the campaign's original 2014 finance filings and the state's contribution database, which the coverage cited for totals but did not compare with Epstein's figures. Together they show a fare that was split on paper, with the campaign's share paid in full and Epstein's share arriving as two corporate contributions.

**Not previously reported.** The comparison of Epstein's "10 4" instruction with the two September 1 contributions, the campaign's September 5 payment date, and the circulation of the attorney general's office address do not appear in the coverage reviewed for this report (NM Political Report, Feb 17 and 23, 2026; Albuquerque Journal, Feb 17; ICT, Feb 19; KOB, Feb 19 and Feb 28; Reuters, Mar 21; The Daily Beast, Apr 16, 2019; Santa Fe New Mexican, Sept 9 and 10, 2014). Searches run September 3, 2026 for `"two diffent entities"` return only verbatim reprints of the Journal's quotation; `"Gary King" "10,400"`, `"take care of gary king office"`, and `"Villagra Building" Epstein "Gary King"` return nothing.

## I. The request

### August 21, 2014 · "possibly using your plane"

Epstein's assistant told him on Thursday, August 21, that King had asked about his plane:

> Please call Gary King when you have a moment...He said he was returning your call but also wanted to speak to you about possibly using your plane to get him from Sante Fe evening of Sept 8th (around 8pm) so he could make a breakfast in DC on Sept 9th...he says he can pay for the fuel and landing/airport fees...He said another option might be to take the Jet Blue red eye flight to NY and then borrow your plane to go to DC from NY...that could work for him as well... ([EFTA00364267](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364267))

As relayed by the assistant, King proposed two routings, both on Epstein's aircraft, and offered to cover fuel and fees, and he was returning Epstein's call. NM Political Report quoted the first half of this message in February. The fuel offer and the second routing were not part of that coverage.

Epstein's own plane did not make the trip. At 4:59 pm the same day he wrote to his pilot, **Larry Visoski**, "gary king needs a cheap charter from sfe , to wash dc. on the eve of sept 8 at 8 pm." Visoski came back that night with a Learjet 55 at "$26,875 all fees and taxes included" and said he was waiting on other operators. Epstein replied "check empty leg" ([EFTA00650515](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00650515), [EFTA00995439](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00995439)). By the next afternoon Visoski was pricing a Hawker with a broker at Injet, an ACP Jets company, and discussing whether Santa Fe's altitude and evening temperatures would allow a nonstop to Dulles ([EFTA00364053](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364053) pp.3–4). The Albuquerque Journal carried the "cheap charter" line in February and dated the email August 22.

## II. The arrangement, Monday, August 25

### 11:26 am · Epstein's instruction

Epstein wrote to his accountant **Richard Kahn** and to Visoski:

> lets orgainze the jet for gary king he can pay 11.6 we can arrange to pay through two diffent entities 10 4 please call his gyy chris khoury ([EFTA00364043](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364043))

**Chris Khoury** was the campaign's finance director. His name and phone number appear on the campaign's own solicitation letters to Epstein ([EFTA00367846](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00367846)). Visoski forwarded Epstein's message inside the office at 11:52 am with the question "who is Chris Khoury? and who is Gary King, lol". A colleague whose name is redacted answered five minutes later: "not sure on Chris Khoury! Gary King is running for Gov. of New Mexico! He has an early meeting in Washington DC on Sept. 9th" ([EFTA00364043](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364043)).

### 11:34 am · "my boss approved"

Visoski told the broker to proceed before the written quote arrived. "Ok Let's confirm the trip, my boss approved, Temps have been in the 57 degrees Pls fwd invoice for Sept 9th SAF-IAD" ([EFTA00364053](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364053) p.2). The "Sept 9th" was a slip; when Visoski later wrote that the date "has been changed", the broker replied "a bit confused, we've only discussed 9/8", and the quote itself carried September 8.

### 11:42 am · The quote

Injet sent quotation #23274 for a Hawker 800XP, Santa Fe to Dulles on September 8, with Larry Visoski and Jege LLC in the "Prepared For" field. Subtotal $20,523.40, tax $1,537.50, **quote total $22,060.90** ([EFTA00364041](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364041) p.2; duplicate at [EFTA00364038](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364038)). The quote's itinerary showed a 9:00 am departure; the campaign wanted 8:00 pm, and the operator's final trip sheet carries 8:00 pm ([EFTA00310528](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00310528)).

### 11:45 to 11:52 am · The attorney general's office, and "office and $"

Visoski circulated the quote to Kahn and to Epstein's assistant as "Charter for Gary King., Sept 9th 8pm departure Santefe to Washington DC IAD Dulles airport" ([EFTA00364038](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364038)). The assistant replied at 11:50 with contact details for the passenger:

> oh great! So Jeffrey is helping out Gary? Do I need to do anything here? Let me pass along Gary King's info:
> Attorney General Gary King
> 408 Galisteo Street
> Villagra Building
> Santa Fe New Mexico 87501
> Victoria is Gary's assistant ([EFTA00364041](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364041))

The Villagra Building at 408 Galisteo Street is the Office of the Attorney General ([New Mexico Department of Justice, contact page](https://nmdoj.gov/contact-us/)). The details Epstein's office circulated for a campaign charter were the state office and an assistant identified only as "Victoria", not the campaign's post office box in Moriarty that appears on the June 13 and June 17 solicitation letters. The operator's final trip sheet lists Visoski, not King's office, as the contact, so the address stayed inside Epstein's chain ([EFTA00310528](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00310528)). Why the state office was the reference point is not explained in any document.

Kahn answered at 11:52:

> i will take care of gary king office and $ ([EFTA00364041](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364041))

Kahn signed as HBRK Associates Inc., 575 Lexington Avenue. His firm had given King's campaign $5,200 nine weeks earlier (Part III).

### 12:59 pm · "Lead passenger Gary King"

Visoski corrected the itinerary with the broker and named the passenger. "Departure date has been changed, does this pose an issue? Pax wants to depart Sept 8th evening estimate 8pm SAF-IAD Total 4 to 5 Pax total., Lead passenger Gary King., Pls cc my NYC office Mr. Richard Kahn CFO" ([EFTA00364053](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364053) pp.1–2).

## III. The money

### June · The campaign explains how corporations can give

The charter sits inside a longer money sequence. King won the Democratic primary on June 3 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_New_Mexico_gubernatorial_election)). On June 5 Epstein asked his assistant to reach King, the assistant spoke with King's assistant Victoria, and that afternoon the campaign emailed Epstein's office a letter over King's signature, which his assistant forwarded to him the next morning. "Thank you for the opportunity to speak with you today … I also want to thank you for your personal commitment to raise $50,000.00 for my campaign" ([EFTA00368519](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00368519); the call sequence is at [EFTA00368401](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00368401) and [EFTA00368431](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00368431)).

A June 13 version of the same letter, re-addressed "C/O Darrin Indyke," added a paragraph.

> Under New Mexico campaign finance law individuals, PACs and corporations can contribute up to $5,200 per election cycle. Each separate corporation is considered to be a separate giver and can give up to the maximum allowed, as long as the entity was not formed solely for the purpose of giving. ([EFTA00304889](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00304889))

On June 17 at 12:06 pm, Epstein's lawyer **Darren Indyke** flagged that King's platform page was titled "CRIMES AGAINST CHILDREN" and asked whether Epstein risked "additional news coverage regarding his accepting of your contributions." Epstein answered at 12:24 pm, "good catch, lets use co s or trusts" ([EFTA00870192](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00870192)). At 7:34 pm that evening the campaign sent the letter a third time, again with the corporations paragraph, under the subject line "Information on PAC contributions" ([EFTA00367846](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00367846)). The next morning it went to Indyke and Kahn ([EFTA00367856](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00367856)). NM Political Report [described](https://nmpoliticalreport.com/2026/02/23/gary-king-campaigned-on-his-record-prosecuting-child-sex-offenders-then-invited-epstein-to-lunch-and-accepted-epstein-donations-through-companies-designed-to-avoid-press-coverage-for-king/) this language and the Indyke exchange but dated the letter June 18. The corporations paragraph existed by June 13, four days before Indyke raised the press question.

State records show what followed. The Accountability Project's copy of New Mexico's legacy campaign finance database ([dataset 392](https://publicaccountability.org/datasets/392/new-mexico-contr/)) and the campaign's Fourth Primary report, submitted July 3 and preserved on [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/doc/232519192/Gary-King-fourth-primary-campaign-finance-report), list **Darren Indyke** personally, $5,200 on June 17; **HBRK Associates Inc.**, $5,200 on June 23; and **Neptune, LLC**, $5,200 on June 23. Neptune's check is in the corpus, dated June 17, the day of "co s or trusts" ([EFTA01348769](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA01348769), page image). The Indyke and HBRK contributions were reported by [The Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/jeffrey-epstein-has-a-secret-charity-heres-who-it-gave-money-to/) in April 2019.

Four more Red Hook entities gave $5,000 each on July 7. The campaign's First General report, submitted September 8 and preserved on [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/doc/239102945/Gary-King-9-18-14-Campaign-Finance-Report), records them as JEGE, LLC; FT RealEstate; Laurel, Inc.; and Nautilus, Inc. The version of the First General report carried in the state database, filed October 14, labels the July JEGE gift "JEGE, Inc.", and the campaign's refund list uses the same two names.

### August 27 to September 1 · $10,400 through two entities

Two days after Epstein's "10 4" instruction, **JEGE, LLC** wrote check #1037 for $5,200 to "Gary King for Governor," drawn on Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas and signed "Darren Indyke" on the authorized-signature line. The bank's processing stamps read September 5 and September 8 ([EFTA01348584](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA01348584), page image).

The campaign's First General report, covering through September 1, records two contributions dated **September 1** on Form B1 ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/doc/239102945/Gary-King-9-18-14-Campaign-Finance-Report); the same rows appear in [dataset 392](https://publicaccountability.org/datasets/392/new-mexico-contr/)).

> 9/1/2014 JEGE, LLC - 6100 Red Hook Quarter B3, St Thomas VI 00802 … $5,200.00
> 9/1/2014 Maple, Inc - 6100 Red Hook Quarter suite B-3, St Thomas VI 00802 … $5,200.00

Two entities. $10,400. Nothing else from any Epstein-linked source is recorded between August 25 and the flight.

Earlier coverage could not see the match because NM Political Report gave Maple's contribution as $5,000 rather than $5,200. The campaign's own filing, the state database, and the Santa Fe New Mexican's original September 9, 2014 [tally](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/news/local_news/king-donors-address-linked-to-sex-offender/article_705feb90-3868-11e4-a341-0017a43b2370.html) all say $5,200.

### September 5 · The campaign pays the full fare

The Second General report, submitted October 14 and covering September 2 through October 6 ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/doc/243013285/Gary-King-10-14-14-Campaign-Finance-Report), Form C), lists one payment to the operator.

> 9/5/2014 Injet - 4145 Southern, Coral Gables FL 00000 … private plane … $22,060.90

That is the quoted fare to the cent, reported paid by the campaign three days before departure and four days before the first story about the donors. The full-fare payment was not a reaction to press coverage.

### September 8 · The flight

Kahn checked in with Khoury at 8:25 am. "please confirm that everything is in order for flight tonight." Khoury: "We should be all set." Kahn: "have a good trip and thanks for your help coordinating." Khoury: "Thank you for all of your help" ([EFTA00363159](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00363159)). Kahn had forwarded the operator's trip sheet to Khoury the night before with the note "Peter please communicate directly with Chris" ([EFTA00363574](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00363574)). An assistant emailed Epstein a reminder that morning, "Gary King to take charter tonight from Sante fe to Washington" ([EFTA00363588](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00363588)).

The trip sheet names the client as Jege LLC, the contact as Larry Visoski, and the aircraft as a Hawker 800XP, N225RP, departing Santa Fe Municipal at 8:00 pm MDT and arriving Dulles at 1:16 am EDT. The passenger manifest lists Gary King, Deb Haaland, Chris Khoury, and two other campaign staffers ([EFTA00310528](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00310528)).

## IV. The arithmetic

| Epstein, Aug 25, 11:26 am | Record |
|---|---|
| "he can pay 11.6" | Campaign reports paying Injet $22,060.90 on Sept 5 |
| "two diffent entities 10 4" | JEGE LLC $5,200 + Maple Inc $5,200, both recorded Sept 1, which is **$10,400** |
| 11.6 + 10.4 | **22.0**, against a fare of $22,060.90 |

Epstein's message preceded the written quote by sixteen minutes, and the broker had told Visoski on August 22 that "we can do it for about $22K with all taxes and fees included" ([EFTA00364053](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00364053) p.4), so the figures he used were round ones for a fare of about $22,000. The sum of his two numbers is $22,000; the fare came in at $22,060.90, a gap of $60.90. Read on its own terms, the message proposes that King's side bear roughly $11,600 and that Epstein's side put in $10,400 through two companies. What the filings then show is two contributions totaling exactly $10,400 from two of his companies, recorded one week before the flight, and the campaign paying the operator everything.

That is consistent with the split Epstein described. It does not establish that the contributions were made or received as fare money. No campaign email says so, and none in the DOJ release discusses the contributions at all. Khoury coordinated the flight with Kahn, and the corporations letter directed questions to Khoury. The records do not show that he, or anyone else at the campaign, linked the September contributions to the charter. The Journal's [readers](https://www.abqjournal.com/news/deb-haaland-rode-on-jet-chartered-by-epstein-in-2014-for-running-mate-gary-king/2982850) saw the email in February. The coverage reviewed for this report did not place the filings beside it.

## V. After the story

### September 9 and 10 · The New Mexican, then the AP calls St. Thomas

On September 9 the Santa Fe New Mexican [reported](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/news/local_news/king-donors-address-linked-to-sex-offender/article_705feb90-3868-11e4-a341-0017a43b2370.html) that companies at Epstein's Virgin Islands address had given King "more than $35,000", itemizing six companies whose gifts total $35,600, a story Deutsche Bank later filed as negative media in its own review of JEGE ([EFTA01296377](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA01296377) pp.2–3). The Associated Press called Epstein's Virgin Islands office the same day. The message reached Epstein through **Cecile de Jongh**, the wife of the sitting governor of the Virgin Islands and the manager of Epstein's local companies (see the [Plaskett investigation](PLASKETT_EPSTEIN_INVESTIGATION.md)). "Barry Massey from the AP called here for you yesterday re Gary King. We just took a message" ([EFTA00630939](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00630939)). The AP [story](https://www.wtxl.com/news/gary-king-refunding-sex-offender-s-contributions/article_49e95023-be8b-5b61-9395-b46eafff2ab5.html) ran September 9. King's statement said his fundraising staff "missed this in the vetting process" ([Santa Fe New Mexican, Sept 10, 2014](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/news/local_news/gary-king-says-he-ll-return-cash-linked-to-sex-offender/article_0cb7a32c-c0f1-51c6-b722-33dcff363b71.html)).

### September 12 · Seven reported refunds, and two contributions untouched

The Second General report lists seven "Refunded Contribution" entries, all dated September 12, 2014, for Maple $5,200; JEGE, LLC $5,200; FT RealEstate $5,000; Laurel $5,000; Nautilus $5,000; JEGE, Inc. $5,000; and Neptune $5,200 ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/doc/243013285/Gary-King-10-14-14-Campaign-Finance-Report), Form C). They total $35,600. The corpus holds one of the physical checks. "Gary King 2014" check #1631 to Laurel, Inc., $5,000, is dated 9-12-14 and carries Gary King's signature ([EFTA01271585](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA01271585) p.190, page image).

In the Second and Third General reports, the two later 2014 filings examined for this report ([Scribd, Oct 30](https://www.scribd.com/doc/245041110/Gary-King-10-30-14-Campaign-Finance-Report)), no refund to Darren Indyke or to HBRK Associates appears. The state database records contributions only and cannot show refunds. King's public pledge, as quoted by the [Santa Fe New Mexican on September 10, 2014](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/news/local_news/gary-king-says-he-ll-return-cash-linked-to-sex-offender/article_0cb7a32c-c0f1-51c6-b722-33dcff363b71.html), covered "the companies in question." The $10,400 from Epstein's lawyer and Epstein's accountant, given in the same June week the corporations letter went out, is not among the reported refunds.

The 2014 total from Epstein's circle was therefore $46,000 rather than the $35,600 reported at the time. It came in nine contributions, eight from entities (seven from the Red Hook companies, counting JEGE's July and September gifts separately, plus HBRK Associates) and one from Indyke personally.

### October 27 · Laurel deposits King's check

Laurel, Inc. deposited King's September 12 refund on October 27, six weeks later, at First Bank Virgin Islands ([EFTA01271585](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA01271585) pp.189–190). Whether the other six refund checks were deposited is not visible in the corpus.

## VII. What is missing

- **The Maple check.** JEGE's $5,200 check is in the corpus. Maple's $5,200 check, recorded the same day, is not. Maple's bank records would show the date it was written.
- **Whether the other six refunds cleared, and whether Indyke or HBRK were ever refunded.** Only Laurel's deposit is in the corpus, and only the 2014 filings through October 30 were examined. Deutsche Bank and First Bank statements for JEGE, Maple, Neptune, FT Real Estate and Nautilus for October through December 2014, and the campaign's 2015 filings, would settle both questions.
- **The campaign's understanding.** No campaign email about the charter is in the DOJ release beyond Khoury's replies to Kahn. Whether anyone at the campaign treated the September 1 contributions as connected to the fare is unknown.
- **The state office.** Why Epstein's assistant circulated the attorney general's Villagra Building address, rather than the campaign's, is not explained in any document.
- **The aircraft and the briefing.** Who owned N225RP, and what King and Haaland were told about who had arranged the charter, are not in the record.
- **Later contacts.** Nine and ten days after the story, an unidentified "Jim Noel" was calling Epstein's office about King, the second message copied to Indyke, and someone using a different number called him back ([EFTA00362315](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00362315), [EFTA00362357](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA00362357)). His identity is unknown.
